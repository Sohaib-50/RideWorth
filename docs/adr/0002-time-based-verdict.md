# Time-based verdict

A flat cash cutoff treats a 15-minute drop and an hour-long crawl as the same job. Take means Net is at least 100 PKR for every 15 minutes the driver will spend driving, including the deadhead when his current location is known. Thin means Net is zero or positive but under that bar, which still covers a relocate or a slow hour. Skip means Net is negative. The minutes and the bar are shown with the verdict.
